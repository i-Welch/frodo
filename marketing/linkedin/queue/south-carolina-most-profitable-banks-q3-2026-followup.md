Farmers and Merchants Bank of South Carolina in Holly Hill paid 0.27% of earning assets for its funding in the first nine months of 2026. At the end of 2023, it paid 0.17%.

Over the same stretch, the median South Carolina bank went from 1.13% to 1.57%.

The call report shows why. Of Holly Hill's deposits, 42.6% pay no interest, and only 8.2% sit in time deposits.

It is one of seven South Carolina banks on both of our top-ten lists, highest return on assets and lowest efficiency ratio. In Georgia in June, four banks made both.

The efficiency leaders spend a median 52.4 cents to earn a dollar of revenue, against 60.8 cents at the median bank, and they pay 1.01% for funding against 1.57%.
