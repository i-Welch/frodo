"We're on SilverLake. Can we just turn on digital lending?"

Not from the core. SilverLake (500+ banks) and Symitar (700+ credit unions) are systems of record. The loan application, the decision and the income verification sit on top.

What Jack Henry sells for that today:

LoanVantage, its loan origination system, strongest in commercial lending
OpenAnywhere, for account opening, with consumer loans listed as coming soon
A reseller deal with MeridianLink, signed August 2025, for consumer and mortgage lending

And what's next: Jack Henry Origination, a forthcoming loan and deposit origination product planned for its cloud-native Jack Henry Platform. No general availability date yet.

If you're choosing a lending tool now, ask what overlaps with what you'll get through LoanVantage or OpenAnywhere later.
