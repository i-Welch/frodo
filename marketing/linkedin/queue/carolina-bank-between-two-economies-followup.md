A 20% capital ratio is not a trophy. It is optionality.

Carolina Bank & Trust holds a 20.14% Tier 1 risk-based capital ratio, more than double the 8% regulators require for a well-capitalized bank. That much capital is expensive in good times. It also means the bank can absorb a sawmill closure in its home county without blinking, and move fast if Florence's paused battery plant restarts.

When demand does arrive, the binding constraint will not be capital. It will be how many loan files the team can process in a week.

Today, only its mortgages have an online application, on a third-party portal. Home equity, personal, auto and business loans start at a branch.

What limits your bank first when demand jumps: capital, or file throughput?
