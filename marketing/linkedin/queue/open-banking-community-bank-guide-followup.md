Three places open banking pays off at a community bank today, with or without a final 1033 rule.

Income verification. Verify income from deposit data when the borrower connects an account, instead of chasing pay stubs for days.

Account funding. Instant account verification lets a new customer fund the account during opening, instead of waiting days for micro-deposits.

Cash flow underwriting. Transaction data helps with thin-file consumers and small businesses that have revenue but little W-2 history.

One free first step: if your bank has never looked at how its customers connect to fintech apps, Plaid's Data Partner Dashboard is now open to community banks and credit unions.
