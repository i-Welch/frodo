BankMiami opened in March 2025. By June 30, 2026 it held $317.9 million in assets, up from $140.9 million nine months earlier.

It was still losing money: $921,000 in the first half. Growth comes first for a de novo. Profit follows if the bank gets through the three-year de novo period.

The 2026 class starts further back. Echelon Bank in Clearwater reported $52.2 million in assets but only $3.8 million in net loans at June 30.

And Tampa Bay is about to get crowded. Echelon's FDIC business plan targets small and medium-sized businesses and individuals in the Tampa metro. New South Bank, approved September 28, names the same customers in Hillsborough County.

Tampa Bay bankers: how are you defending your small business book against two new local banks at once?
