Section 1033 is on hold. Open banking is not.

Where the rule stands: finalized in October 2024, enforcement barred by a Kentucky court a year later, and the CFPB rewriting it since August 2025. The biggest reported change is fees. The 2024 rule banned charging for data access. The rewrite is reported to allow fees after a set number of free requests.

ICBA has asked for every community bank under $10 billion to be exempt from the developer interface mandate. The 2024 rule exempted only banks at or below $850 million.

Meanwhile, at community banks:

MeridianLink put Plaid Income inside MeridianLink Consumer in April. Borrowers verify income by connecting an account instead of uploading pay stubs.

Jack Henry has had API data agreements with Plaid, Finicity, Yodlee and Akoya since 2020 and 2021.

Plaid opened its Data Partner Dashboard to community banks and credit unions last November, so they can fix their own connection issues.

The rule will settle the threshold and the fees. It will not change the direction.
