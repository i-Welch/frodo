Georgia's ten most profitable community banks earned a median 2.75% return on assets in the first half of 2026. The median Georgia bank earned 1.56%.

We pulled the June 30 FDIC call reports for all 122 banks headquartered in the state to see why.

Start with taxes. Eight of the ten are Subchapter S corporations, which report little or no income tax. In the full ranking pool, about one bank in four is.

Before taxes, the gap still holds: a median 3.02% for the leaders, 1.88% for the median bank.

The rest is cheap money. The leaders pay 0.95% of earning assets for funding, against 1.58% statewide, and 32.2% of their deposits pay no interest, against 25.3%. Their margin is 5.37%, against 4.50%.

They do not run lean. The leaders carry $6.7 million of assets per employee; the state median is $6.8 million.

Georgia's most efficient banks are mostly a different crowd. Only four banks make both top-ten lists, and the efficiency leaders win on staffing, not deposits.

How much of your margin rests on deposits that pay nothing, and how fast can a new customer open one?
