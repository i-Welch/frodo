Applications started is a vanity metric in digital account opening.

The numbers that matter are the funded-account rate and the deposit balances 30 days later. Ask every reference customer for those two, not for application volume.

Then time yourself. Open an account at your own bank on your phone with a stopwatch running, then do the same at a neobank. The gap between the two is your requirements document.

And ask the fraud question first: what is checked at the source before an account can be funded? "We integrate with a fraud tool" is a different answer than "these checks run on every application."

What is your bank's funded-account rate on online applications?
