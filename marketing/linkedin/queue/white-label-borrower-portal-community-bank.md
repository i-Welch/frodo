24% of small banks took small business loan applications through an online portal in the FDIC's latest Small Business Lending Survey. Large banks: 42%.

Only 6% of all banks let a borrower finish every step online. 60% offered no online steps at all.

The FDIC's own read is skeptical. Only 36% of banks rated online portals very valuable, against 94% for branches, and the agency concluded banks without one are not at a significant disadvantage.

That is fair. A portal does not replace a banker who knows the business.

But that is the wrong comparison. A portal takes over the part of the relationship nobody enjoys: chasing a missing tax return, re-sending a document request, answering "is my file complete?" The banker gets that time back for the conversations that need a banker.

And the survey asked banks what they value, not the business owner who applied somewhere else because your application required a branch visit.
