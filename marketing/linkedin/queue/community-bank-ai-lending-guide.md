SR 11-7 is gone. On April 17, the Fed, OCC and FDIC replaced it with revised model risk guidance: SR 26-2, OCC Bulletin 2026-13 and FIL-15-2026.

Three things community bankers should know about it.

It is explicitly proportional. It builds on the OCC's October 2025 bulletin, which said community banks are not required to validate models annually.

Vendor models are still your models. Licensing an AI tool does not move the governance responsibility to the fintech that built it.

Generative and agentic AI are outside its scope. The agencies left those to a separate process. So for an AI tool that reads loan documents, there is no single checklist yet, and examiners will lean on general safety-and-soundness and third-party risk expectations.

Meanwhile 49% of banks had deployed generative AI going into 2026, triple the year before, per Cornerstone Advisors.

The practical answer is to build the artifacts an examiner will ask for anyway: a source-page citation for every figure the AI extracts, preserved override history, and a named model risk owner.
