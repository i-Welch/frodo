How does a bank stay profitable while paying more interest than it earns?

United Fidelity Bank did it for two years. Net interest income turned negative in 2023 and stayed negative: minus $18.4 million in 2023, minus $49.5 million in 2024, minus $40.2 million in 2025.

Fee income covered the gap. $210.8 million in 2022, then $163.9 million, $150.2 million and $118.8 million.

Net income followed it down: $153.2 million, $46.3 million, $9.9 million, then a loss.

By September 2026, 70.6% of its loans were noncurrent. The OCC closed it on November 27.

Fee income can cover a negative margin for a while. As that income shrinks, the bank has to make money on its loans again, and this bank was 89% multifamily.
