A 125-year-old bank in Mount Olive, NC cut its efficiency ratio by 17 points in two years.

Southern Bank and Trust, from its FDIC call reports:

Efficiency ratio: 69.4% in Q1 2024, 52.6% in the first half of 2026
Net interest margin: 2.68% to 3.70%
First-half net income: $36.8M, up 57%
ROA 1.40%, ROE 17.1%

Most of it came from the margin. Net interest income grew 24% while expenses grew about 7%.

Then, on October 19, it moved every customer to a new digital banking platform.

Deposits, HELOCs and mortgages can already start online. Personal, auto and business loans still start with a phone call or a contact form.

Margin gains don't repeat forever. The next points come from closing more loans with the same 603 people.
