27 companies are waiting on FDIC deposit insurance right now.

We added the full FDIC pending list to De Novo Watch, every state, dated and sourced. Utah has six applicants and Florida five. The newest filings are Upgrade Bank (Oct. 9) and Final Finance Bank (Oct. 8). The oldest, OneMain Bank, has been waiting since March 2025.

The mix is the story. Community bank organizers like Glades Bank and Trust in Plantation, Florida, sit in the same queue as PayPal, Klarna, Revolut, Nubank, Affirm and Upstart.

Glades also named its vendors this month: CSI's NuPoint core and Apiture for digital and business banking, with a launch target of the first quarter of 2027, pending approval.

For community bankers: which of these charters worries you most, the fintech lender or the new bank down the street?
