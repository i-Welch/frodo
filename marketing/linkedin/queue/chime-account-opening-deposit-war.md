Chime is not a bank.

Its accounts are held at The Bancorp Bank and Stride Bank. It ended June with 10.4 million active members, up 20% in a year, without a single branch. The charter was never the advantage. The front door was.

Chime advertises opening an account in under two minutes, with no ChexSystems check, no minimum balance and no monthly fee.

That speed has a cost. In 2024 the CFPB ordered Chime to pay $3.25 million plus redress for delaying refunds on closed accounts, in thousands of cases by more than 90 days. A community bank's slower process comes with a person who can fix a problem on the spot.

But the customers are moving anyway. J.D. Power's 2026 study found 20% of retail bank customers moved money away from their primary bank in three months. Cornerstone Advisors puts digital banks and fintechs at 44% of new checking accounts opened in 2024.

Nobody closes the old account. They open a new one somewhere easier, and the balance drifts.
