We followed every "Apply now" button at 32 Southeast banks and credit unions to see which vendor was behind it.

Mortgage is the most standardized layer. 16 of the 32 send applicants to an ICE Encompass portal, and 4 more to Finastra's Mortgagebot.

Digital banking is led by Jack Henry's Banno (7), then Fiserv (4) and Q2 (2).

Consumer lending and account opening are the most scattered. MeridianLink shows up most, but many institutions have no online consumer loan application at all, and several hand borrowers to a different vendor domain for every product.

We put the whole dataset in a free tracker, every institution, every layer, sourced to its live site.

For bankers: how many different vendor domains does one of your borrowers touch from first click to closing?
