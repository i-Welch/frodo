South Carolina's ten most profitable community banks earned a median 1.64% return on assets through September 30. The median bank in the state earned 1.13%.

They did not earn more on their loans. Their loans and securities yield a median 5.07%, against 5.29% at the median bank.

The edge is funding. The leaders pay 0.92% of earning assets for their money. The median South Carolina bank pays 1.57%.

On the median bank's $510 million of earning assets, that gap is worth about $3.3 million a year.

At the median leader, 28.4% of deposits pay no interest, against 21.4% statewide. Time deposits are 18.2% of their deposits, against 25.0%. One of the ten uses brokered deposits; 14 of the state's 42 banks do.

The gap has widened. At the end of 2023 it was 34 basis points. Now it is 65.

This is not Georgia's tax story, either. Only 4 of South Carolina's 42 banks are S corporations, and one makes the top ten.
