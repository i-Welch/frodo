In June 2025, AESC paused construction on its $1.6 billion EV battery plant in Florence, SC. In August, Canfor permanently closed its sawmill one county over in Darlington.

Carolina Bank & Trust, headquartered in Lamar since 1936, sits between the two. Its first half of 2026, from the FDIC call report:

ROA 1.85%, up from 1.49%
Efficiency ratio 43.2%, down from 50.2%
Net interest margin 4.17%
Net income $7.9M, up 34.5%
Tier 1 risk-based capital 20.14%
Foreclosed real estate: zero

92 people, $845 million in assets, 14 offices, one family running it for 90 years.

The battery economy is still a promise, not a payroll. The bank's results show it never needed the plant to perform.
