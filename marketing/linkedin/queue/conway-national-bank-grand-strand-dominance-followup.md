A coastal retiree is not a hard credit. They are a slow file.

Social Security. A pension from a former employer. A brokerage account paying dividends. A rental property in another state. No W-2, and documentation spread across several institutions.

Verifying that by hand takes weeks. On the Grand Strand, that borrower is arriving in volume, often buying remotely and coordinating through a local agent.

Conway National Bank runs a 47.9% efficiency ratio, one of the leanest cost bases you will find. Lean banks feel manual document work the most: every hour a processor spends chasing a statement shows up in the ratio.

As of October, CNB's mortgage page offers a payment calculator but no online application.

How long does a multi-income retiree file take at your bank?
