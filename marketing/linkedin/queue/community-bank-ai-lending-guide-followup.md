Adverse action is the one AI rule that does not bend for bank size.

Regulation B requires a lender to give a declined applicant the specific principal reasons. That does not loosen because an algorithm influenced the decision. "The model scored you low" is not a reason.

That is why the safest first AI projects in lending do not decide credit at all. Fraud models that flag files for human review. Document extraction that spreads a financial statement and links every number back to its source page.

The AI does the reading and the arithmetic. The underwriter makes the call, with the evidence in front of them.

Where would document extraction save your underwriters the most time?
