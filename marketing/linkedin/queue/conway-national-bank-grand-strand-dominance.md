Conway National Bank holds the largest deposit share in Horry County, SC. Ahead of Truist, Wells Fargo and Bank of America. Every year since at least 2021.

12.41% of the county's deposits in June 2026, per the FDIC, the largest of 21 institutions.

And it does it lean. Its first half of 2026, from the call report:
Efficiency ratio 47.9%, down from 52.9%
ROA 1.42%, up from 1.11%
Net interest margin 3.31%, up from 2.98%
Net income $13.9M, up 34.7%
Noncurrent loans 0.02%

The county keeps growing around it: up 17.8% from 2020 to 2024 per the Census Bureau, with permits for 7,331 new homes in 2024 alone.

The interesting number is in Georgetown County. CNB's 27 mortgages there in 2025 averaged about $599,000, nearly three times its Horry County average. That is the Waccamaw Neck: second homes, retirees and buyers purchasing from out of state.
