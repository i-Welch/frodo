Georgia has added six community bank de novos since 2010. One is already gone.

Tandem Bank opened in Tucker in 2019 and merged into Georgia Banking Company on June 1, 2026.

The four survivors from 2019 to 2022 held between $150.6 million and $314.3 million in assets at June 30. That is still well under half the size of Peach State Bank & Trust when United Community bought it this summer in a deal valued at $100.8 million.

The sixth, Private Bank & Trust, opened in Gainesville on September 28. It is the first new Georgia bank since May 2022.

The pattern in the FDIC data: Georgia builds banks slowly and sells the grown ones to regionals.

How many years should a de novo's investors expect to wait for liquidity?
