A 75.9% efficiency ratio and a 23% return on equity. At the same bank.

Countybank in Greenwood, SC, first half of 2026:

Net income $5.4M, up 30%
ROA 1.45%
Noninterest income: 43% of revenue
Gains on loan sales: $3.9M, up 47%

It earns like a mortgage company. Mortgage teams run on people, so the efficiency ratio is high and returns are strong anyway.

The 2025 HMDA data shows where the growth is: 196 home loans in Greenville County, 74 in Greenwood County, 53 in Anderson.

The mortgage team wins borrowers an hour from home with a real application. For a HELOC or an auto loan, the same customer gets a contact form or an unverified application and a callback.
