First Community Bank in Lexington, SC is handing a strong bank to a new team.

In 2026 it:

Closed the Signature Bank of Georgia deal in January and converted systems by mid-March
Grew Q2 net income 46.5%, to $7.6M
Widened its net interest margin for the ninth straight quarter, to 3.51%
Held nonperforming assets to 0.04% of total assets

On January 1, bank CEO Ted Nissen hands the job to two people: Vaughan Dozier as CEO and Drew Painter as president.

What they inherit: 23 offices from the Midlands to Sandy Springs, a loan book that is 57% commercial real estate, and an online lending path that, outside mortgage, is a contact form.

At home, borrowers already know a banker. In Atlanta and Augusta, a new borrower is comparing banks online.
