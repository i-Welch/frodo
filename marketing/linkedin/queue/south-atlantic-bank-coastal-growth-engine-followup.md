At a $1 million average mortgage, every slow file is expensive.

South Atlantic Bank's 2025 mortgages in Beaufort and Charleston counties averaged more than $1 million each, per HMDA. Those buyers are often relocating or buying a second home, comparing lenders at once, and many have applied with a national lender that verified income and assets in minutes.

The bank's digital front door has improved: its mortgage page now links each loan officer's online application. But that application runs on a third-party portal on a separate domain, and HELOCs, personal loans and every commercial product still start with a phone call or a contact form.

On a growing book, the next efficiency gains come from verified files at intake, not more people chasing documents.

How does your bank handle a remote buyer's first application?
