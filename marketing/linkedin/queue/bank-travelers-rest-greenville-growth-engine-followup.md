Greenville's in-migrant homebuyer is not a hard credit. They are a slow file.

A W-2 from an employer in another state. Some side income. Bank accounts at a credit union back home. Verifying that by hand means chasing pay stubs, calling an employer and waiting on statement PDFs, while the contract's closing date gets closer.

Bank of Travelers Rest wrote 108 purchase mortgages in 2025, all conventional. Its consumer loan application is online, which is more than many banks its size offer. But it takes income as typed, with nothing verified at intake, and mortgage applicants are sent to a third-party portal on a separate domain.

Verification that happens before underwriting, with the borrower's consent, does not require a new core or LOS. It layers onto the form that already exists.
