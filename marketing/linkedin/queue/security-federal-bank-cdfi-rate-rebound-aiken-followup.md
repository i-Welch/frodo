Aiken County added 7,962 residents between 2020 and 2023, a 4.71% gain at nearly five times the national rate.

Across the river, Augusta is anchored by Savannah River Site, with more than 10,000 workers, and Fort Eisenhower, home of Army Cyber Command headquarters.

That mix produces exactly the borrowers a CDFI bank serves: retirees on fixed incomes, federal contractors on short assignments, first-time buyers with nontraditional income.

In a segment where credit unions and other mission lenders compete for the same customer, speed to clear-to-close decides who wins the house. Realtors and builders remember which lenders close on time.
