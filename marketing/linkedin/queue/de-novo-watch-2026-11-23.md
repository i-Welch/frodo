The FDIC just approved OneMain's Utah industrial bank, 20 months after the application arrived.

OneMain Bank filed for deposit insurance on March 13, 2025, the oldest application on the FDIC's pending list. The Nov. 17 order sets tough terms: at least $341 million in opening capital and a leverage ratio of at least 15%. The bank will make personal loans, auto loans and credit cards, funded mostly by brokered deposits, and sell most of its loans to OneMain within two days. OneMain expects to open in about a year.

Two days later, the OCC conditionally approved UK lender OakNorth's purchase of Community Unity Bank in Birmingham, Michigan, a bank chartered in 2023. OakNorth enters U.S. banking by buying an existing charter instead of filing a de novo.

Nubank, filed in October 2025, is now the longest wait on the FDIC list.

For bankers and organizers: is buying a young charter now faster than the de novo queue?
