Security Federal Bank cut its efficiency ratio from 75.35% to 70.44% in a year. The rate rebound did most of the work.

The Aiken, SC bank has been a certified CDFI since 2010 and runs 19 branches across the Aiken-Augusta-Columbia corridor. Its first-half 2026 call report shows ROA up to 0.86% from 0.65%, and net interest margin up to 3.30% from 3.02%.

The next five points of efficiency have to come from operations.

That is harder for a CDFI than for most banks. The borrowers it exists to serve rarely have simple files: gig and seasonal income, Schedule C self-employment, multiple part-time jobs. Manual document collection on those files can run two to three times longer than a conventional loan.

Security Federal already runs a modern mortgage portal on ICE's Encompass Consumer Connect. What its public pages do not show is automated income, employment or asset verification at the point of application.
