Sezzle just filed for FDIC deposit insurance, which makes three buy now, pay later lenders waiting on the FDIC.

The FDIC received the Sezzle Bank, National Association application on Oct. 22. Sezzle is also seeking a national bank charter from the OCC and expects the reviews to take 12 to 18 months. It first pursued a Utah industrial loan company license, then switched after New York, Illinois and Oregon adopted new BNPL rules.

Affirm Bank (filed Jan. 23) and Klarna Bank USA (July 6) are already in the queue.

The same week, the FDIC approved Venture Coast Bank, a Palo Alto community bank, with at least $30 million in opening capital.

That brings 2026 deposit insurance filings to 29 nationally through Oct. 27. Florida still leads with six.

For community bankers: when BNPL lenders can take insured deposits, does that change how you price consumer credit?
