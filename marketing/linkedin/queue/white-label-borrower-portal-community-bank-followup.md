The hardest step to take online is the last one.

In the FDIC's latest Small Business Lending Survey, a quarter of banks took applications through an online portal. Only 13% could have an approved loan's closing documents signed online.

When you evaluate a borrower portal, watch where the borrower leaves your brand. Identity checks, document upload and e-signature are often handed to a third-party URL. Every unfamiliar domain is a moment where a cautious business owner stops and wonders whether it is a phishing page. If they abandon, they often do not know whether to call you or the vendor.

Four questions for any vendor:
Is the integration with your core a production integration with community bank references?
Can your compliance team and examiners use its audit trail?
Does it save partial applications and follow up with borrowers who stop?
How long did the last community bank in your asset range actually take to launch?

Which step loses the most borrowers at your bank?
