Five of South Carolina's ten most efficient community banks are in our digital lending audit of 32 Southeast banks and credit unions. Some of them are among the least digital lenders in it.

Relationship banking works. The audit measures something else: how a stranger becomes a relationship.

One finding held across all 32 records. None shows a loan application that confirms income or assets from source data while the applicant fills it in.

Where the form was visible, it collected what the borrower typed. Verification came afterward: a callback within one business day, documents chased after submission.

At five institutions, a mortgage application starts from a loan officer's profile. Pick a person first, then apply.

How much of your lenders' week goes to verifying what the application could have verified itself?
