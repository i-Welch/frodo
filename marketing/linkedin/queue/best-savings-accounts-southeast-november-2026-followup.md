3.60% at Robinhood, 4.00% at Chime, 3.30% at SoFi. Each of those savings rates comes with a condition.

Robinhood pays 3.60% only to Robinhood Gold members, at $5 a month, and publishes no rate for anyone else. Net of the subscription, that is 3.00% on $10,000.

Chime pays 1.00% standard and 4.00% with $3,000 or more in direct deposits in 34 days. SoFi pays 0.80% without direct deposit.

Big-bank promotions sit behind balances. Wells Fargo's 3.50% offer needs $25,000. Citi's 3.50% Premium Boost needs $30,000 or more.

Our Southeast savings scorecard ranks the rate a $10,000 balance earns with no strings attached, with some credit for the conditional rate. Four accounts pay 3.55% or more with no strings.
