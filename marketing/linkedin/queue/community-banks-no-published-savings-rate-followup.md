State Employees' Credit Union has 275 branches in North Carolina, more than any bank or credit union on our North Carolina savings scorecard.

It pays 0.25% on savings, and it publishes that rate.

That is the pattern in this month's expansion of the scorecard. Every state now has 10 local banks and credit unions on it: 55 savings accounts in all, 53 ranked.

Among accounts with a no-strings rate, the median is 0.20% at community banks and 0.25% at credit unions. At the big five banks it is 0.04%.

The local institutions that score best pair a published rate with a branch network. In Georgia that is Georgia United, at 1.75% with 14 branches.

Bankers: which matters more to your depositors today, the rate or the branch?
