Fiserv had a rough year. A guidance cut that sent the stock down 44% in a day. A new CEO in June.

If you run Premier, Precision or Portico, the part that matters is smaller: Fiserv says it is not consolidating its cores. You are keeping the one you have.

So the digital lending question is the same as last year. None of the three cores ships with a borrower-facing loan application. That always comes from a separate product, Fiserv's Loan Director or a third party.

The pieces:

Communicator Open is the API layer for Premier, Signature and Precision.
The AppMarket is where pre-integrated vendors live.
The origination system is where the borrower actually applies.

Portico is the outlier. Commercial origination has to come from outside, as with the BAFS BLAST integration announced last December.
