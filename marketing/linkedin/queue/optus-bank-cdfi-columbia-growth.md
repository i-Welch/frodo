At the end of 2018, Optus Bank in Columbia, SC had $65 million in assets.

On June 30, 2026: $764 million. With 46 employees and two branches.

It started in 2020. PayPal made Optus the first recipient of its $530 million commitment to Black and minority-owned businesses, with a $50 million deposit. In 2021, Citi, Wells Fargo, JPMorgan Chase and Bank of America were among seven investors who put in more than $20 million of capital.

The bank put it to work in commercial lending: $198M in commercial real estate and $147M in C&I, against just $14M in home loans.

In July, Optus agreed to merge with Durham's M&F Bank. The result: a $1.27 billion CDFI with 10 branches across both Carolinas, which its owners call the largest African American-owned financial institution in the country.

The next challenge is making a two-state bank feel like one.
