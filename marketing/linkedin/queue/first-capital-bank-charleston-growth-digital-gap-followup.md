First Capital Bank carries $356 million in nonfarm commercial real estate and $95 million in construction loans, and runs at a 53% efficiency ratio.

Lean staffing and a CRE-heavy book are a good combination until deal flow speeds up. A $3 million CRE loan needs tax returns, rent rolls, entity documents and guarantor financials, and when all of it moves by email and courier, diligence alone can take three weeks.

In Charleston's construction market, that is how a well-run bank loses deals it should win.

The bottleneck is file assembly, not credit judgment. Which is the easier problem to fix.

How long does document gathering take on your bank's commercial deals?
