70.4% of the deposits in Elbert County, Georgia sit at one bank: Vallant, headquartered in Elberton.

The bank formed from the Pinnacle and Morris merger also holds 34.3% of Laurens County, home to Dublin and Morris's old main office.

Census estimates show Elbert lost 106 residents in the year to July 2025. Laurens gained 456.

The growth is elsewhere in the footprint. Jackson County added 4,963 people, Houston 3,310 and Hall 3,023. Vallant holds 3.1%, 12.8% and 2.8% of those markets.

Strong share at home, small share where people are arriving.
