MANTL customers have raised more than $40 billion in deposits on the platform, with an average under five minutes to open a retail account.

That is the benchmark a community bank's account opening flow is now measured against.

The vendor map moved this past year. Alkami owns MANTL. CSI completed its acquisition of Apiture in October 2025, so one of the main non-core digital banking options now belongs to a core provider. Candescent serves more than 1,300 banks and credit unions.

The branch is back, too: MANTL says more than a million in-branch applications have been booked through it, and the branch is its fastest-growing channel.

Speed is only half of the evaluation. Banks that opened the channel without verifying at the source learned the other lesson, and some turned online account opening off after a fraud wave.

We wrote a buyer's guide to the field, with a vendor disclosure in the second sentence. Four questions to ask any vendor: what is checked before funding, how heavy is implementation, what is the funded-account rate, and what happens to the roadmap if your core changes.
