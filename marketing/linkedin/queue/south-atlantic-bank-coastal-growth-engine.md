South Atlantic Bank opened in Myrtle Beach in November 2007, right as the financial crisis hit. It is now a $1.98 billion bank with 12 offices from the Grand Strand to Hilton Head.

Its first half of 2026, from the FDIC call report:
Net income $10.4M, up 30%
ROA 1.07%, up from 0.87%
Efficiency ratio 59.2%, down from 68.4% for 2024
Construction loans $266M, up 40% in a year

The surprise is the mortgage geography. Per HMDA, its 2025 loans in Horry County averaged about $394,000. In Charleston County, about $1.09 million. In Beaufort County, home to Hilton Head and Bluffton, about $1.19 million.

All 477 home loans it made in 2024 and 2025 were conventional.
