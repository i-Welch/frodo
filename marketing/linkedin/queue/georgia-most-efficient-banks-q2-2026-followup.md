Classic City Bank in Athens ran $314 million in assets from one office with 22 employees at midyear. The FDIC dates its establishment to November 2020.

That is $14.3 million of assets per employee, more than twice the Georgia median of $6.8 million. It pays those employees about $158,000 each, half again the state median, and still spent 39.8 cents to earn each dollar of revenue.

It is not alone. Georgia's ten most efficient community banks run a median $154 million of assets per office, against $92 million statewide, and spend 0.15% of assets on premises against 0.25%.

A staffing model set on day one can carry a bank a long way.
