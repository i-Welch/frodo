The Fed raised rates 25 basis points in September. The 2-year Treasury had already climbed 136 basis points by the day it voted.

On Wednesday the Fed held at 3.75% to 4.00%, with two members dissenting in favor of another hike. The September hike was the first since July 2023.

The market got there first. The 2-year went from 3.38% in February to 4.70% this week. Freddie Mac's 30-year mortgage average went from 5.98% to 7.40% in early October.

Deposit rates barely moved. The FDIC's national average 12-month CD rate rose from 1.52% in March to 1.76% in October. The national savings rate went from 0.39% to 0.38%.

Depositors are noticing. Money market fund assets hit $7.94 trillion in the week ended October 21.

For community banks in SC, NC, GA and FL, the late-2025 cuts were worth real money: first-half interest expense fell 7.4%, or $150.5 million, from a year earlier. That tailwind is over.
