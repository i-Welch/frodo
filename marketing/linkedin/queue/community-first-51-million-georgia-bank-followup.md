The $51 million Community First Credit Union paid for First Southern Bank equals 13% of its net worth.

A credit union cannot pay in stock. It pays cash, and none of the bank's $32.7 million in equity comes across as credit union net worth.

On June 30 figures, adding First Southern's $347.2 million balance sheet takes Community First's net worth ratio from 12.26% to roughly 11.2% before new earnings. Still well capitalized. Still a real step down.

What it got for that: deposits whose interest expense ran about 1.2% of balances in the first half of 2026, against about 1.8% for the credit union's own shares and deposits.

Community bankers: what would a buyer pay today for your cheapest deposits?
