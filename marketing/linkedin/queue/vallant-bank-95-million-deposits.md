$95.0 million of noninterest-bearing deposits left Vallant Bank in one quarter.

Vallant is the April 1 merger of Pinnacle Bank of Elberton and Morris Bank of Dublin, now the fourth-largest bank headquartered in Georgia. The two banks moved onto one system on June 17.

The earnings worked. Third-quarter net income was $16.7 million, a 1.73% annualized return on assets, on a 4.79% net interest margin.

The funding is the open question. Noninterest-bearing deposits fell 9.1% between June 30 and September 30, about 72% of a $132.0 million drop in total deposits.

The bank covered it with cash. Balances held at other banks fell from $184.8 million to $39.7 million.

The front door is uneven. Consumers can open checking and apply for most loans online. A business can't open a checking account online, and business loans above $50,000 have no online application.

Management says deposits have stabilized. The next two quarters will show it.
