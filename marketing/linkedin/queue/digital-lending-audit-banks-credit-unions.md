We audited the digital lending journeys of 32 Southeast banks and credit unions. Not one of the 27 banks takes a business loan application online.

The servicing side works. All 26 online banking apps we checked do the job for existing customers.

The front door is another story. Of 180 steps a new borrower or depositor would take, 25 passed. Another 96 led to a phone number, an email address, a branch locator or a contact form.

Mortgage is the most digital channel: 27 of 32 take an application online. But 14 send the borrower to a subdomain of mymortgage-online.com, off the institution's own site.

Home equity is the quiet gap. 18 of the 21 banks that market a HELOC take no application online.

Vendors multiply by product. One Georgia bank sends mortgage, auto and checking applicants to four different web addresses.

Credit unions do better. 39% of their new-member steps graded ok, against 9% at the banks.

If a business owner wanted to apply for a $50,000 line of credit at your bank tonight, where would they start?
