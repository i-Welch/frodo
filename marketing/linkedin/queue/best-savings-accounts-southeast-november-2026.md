The big five banks pay a median 0.04% on a $10,000 savings balance. The top-scoring savings accounts in the Southeast pay 3.00% to 4.00% with no conditions.

We scored 39 savings accounts on rate, fees, money movement, in-person access and protections: fintech apps, online banks, the big five, and 24 community banks and credit unions in SC, NC, GA and FL. Every fact comes from the institution's own pages, verified November 26.

X Money scores highest, at 8.9 out of 10. Wealthfront is second at 8.4.

Third is a tie between a Macon, Georgia community bank and a North Carolina credit union. American Pride Bank pays 4.00% with branches in all four states. Coastal Credit Union pays 3.00% at $10,000 with no monthly fee.

Across the 24 local institutions, the median no-strings rate is 0.25%. Nine pay 0.10% or less.

The locals lead on branches. Suncoast has 78 in Florida. Delta Community has 31 in Georgia.

Many fintech headline rates need a direct deposit or a subscription. The scorecard ranks the rate you get without one, and shows both.

Community bankers: what keeps your savings rate where it is, the cost of funds or the work of changing the product?
