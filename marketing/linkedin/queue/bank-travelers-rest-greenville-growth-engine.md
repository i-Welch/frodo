Bank of Travelers Rest has never had a branch outside Greenville County, SC. In 80 years.

Its first half of 2026, from the FDIC call report:
$1.60B in assets, up 5.8% in a year
1.60% annualized ROA, up from 1.42%
Net interest margin 3.27%, up from 3.12%
Net income $12.7M, up 18.5%
Noncurrent loans 0.12%

In July it opened its 11th branch, in Simpsonville. Its first new office in a decade.

The county keeps feeding it. Greenville County had an estimated 583,000 residents in July 2025, about 11% more than in 2020. BTR originated 170 home loans in 2025, 108 of them purchases averaging about $494,000. Every one conventional.

The one number that has barely moved is efficiency: 61% at the end of 2024, 59% now. The revenue side is doing the work.
