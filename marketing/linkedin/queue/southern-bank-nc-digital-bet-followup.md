1,557 home loans. 8 of them VA.

That is Southern Bank and Trust's 2025 HMDA filing. The bank has offices near Seymour Johnson Air Force Base in Goldsboro and across Hampton Roads, home of Naval Station Norfolk.

Skipping VA lending is a common, reasonable choice for a community bank. It has its own rules and servicing work.

But military borrowers who take conventional loans still bring military income. Base pay, housing allowance and special pays sit on a Leave and Earnings Statement, not a pay stub. A PCS move can look like a job change.

The file is harder either way. The question is whether the intake handles it, or a loan officer does by hand.

How does your bank verify military income today?
