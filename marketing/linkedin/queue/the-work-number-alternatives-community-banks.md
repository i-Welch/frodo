Verifying employment on one mortgage with two borrowers can cost $280 through The Work Number. Equifax raised its prices again on January 1, 2026.

That figure comes from the Community Home Lenders of America: $55 to $70 per verification, pulled for each borrower during underwriting and again shortly before closing.

The price is only half the problem for community banks. Database coverage is thinnest exactly where you lend: small employers, farms, municipalities, and the self-employed.

The alternatives look different than they did two years ago. Checkr bought Truework. Experian plugged Workday's payroll data into its own network. Truv and Argyle both deliver consumer-permissioned payroll data that Fannie Mae's DU validation service and Freddie Mac's AIM accept.

No single tool replaces The Work Number one for one. The realistic strategy chains methods: an instant lookup first, a borrower-permissioned connection second, a manual or verbal VOE as the floor.
