Experian says its Verify network now has a path to more than 80 million payroll records, over half of US payroll employees, after adding Workday in September.

"Path to" is a projection, not a current count. Before switching anything, run the lookup against your own recent files and measure the hit rate on your actual borrowers.

That test matters more for community banks than for anyone. A database built from large employers will always miss more often at the farm, the family business, and the county government.

For portfolio loans, the verbal VOE is still free and compliant when done to Fannie Mae's timing and sourcing rules.

How many of your borrowers last quarter could any payroll database have found?
