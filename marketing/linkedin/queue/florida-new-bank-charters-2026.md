From 2011 through 2020, three new banks opened in Florida. In 2026 alone, six groups asked the FDIC to insure a new Florida bank.

That is 6 of at least 28 deposit insurance applications filed nationally this year, more than any other state. Utah is next with five.

We counted every Florida filing, approval and opening from FDIC and OCC records.

Three Florida de novos won FDIC approval in 2026: Echelon Bank in Clearwater, Portrait Bank in Winter Park and New South Bank in Tampa.

Two have opened. The FDIC records nine bank openings nationwide this year, and Florida is the only state with two.

Since 2021, nine new banks have opened in Florida. That is three times the previous decade, in under six years.

The capital floors for the community de novos sit between $23 million and $28 million. Portrait raised $43 million from 256 local investors, 54% above its minimum.

The filers are not all alike. Itaú's proposed Miami bank must hold at least $507 million under its OCC approval. Two Coral Gables applicants have said almost nothing publicly.
