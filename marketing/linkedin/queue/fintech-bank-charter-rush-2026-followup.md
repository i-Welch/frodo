"My job is not about incumbent protection."

That is Comptroller Jonathan Gould in August, describing roughly 40 charter applications in about 18 months. By late September the OCC had approved 27 de novo applicants since January 2025, denied two and returned one.

Approval is not automatic. The OCC denied bunq in August over unclear capitalization and thin credit card experience.

But the direction is clear, and it reaches community banks in an unexpected place: the partner banks that sponsor fintech programs. Mission Lane plans to move its card program to its own bank over three years and become the sole issuer.

For banks with a sponsor business: how much of that fee income assumes the fintech never gets its own charter?
