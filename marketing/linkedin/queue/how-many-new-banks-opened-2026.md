Nine new banks opened in the US in 2026, by the FDIC's own records. That is the most since 2022.

It is probably not the number you have seen. Counts published this year ran from three to eight, depending on who counted, when, and what they decided a new bank is.

The biggest swing factor is one bank. Erebor, a technology-focused national bank in Columbus, held $8.67 billion in assets at September 30. The other eight new banks held $490 million combined.

Approvals get counted as openings, too. The OCC lists 21 national bank charter approvals for 2026. Fifteen banks the FDIC approved this year had still not opened.

And openings are only half the ledger. Call report filers fell from 4,411 at the end of 2025 to 4,284 at September 30. Add back the nine and roughly 136 charters left the industry in nine months, four of them through failures.

That is about 15 charters out for every one in.

If you were starting a bank in 2027, what would you build before opening day?
