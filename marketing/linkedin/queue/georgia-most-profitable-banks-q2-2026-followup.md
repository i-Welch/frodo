Georgia's most efficient community banks pay their people more than the median bank does: about $110,600 per employee in salaries and benefits, against $102,800.

They just need fewer of them. The ten leaders run $9.5 million of assets per employee, against $6.8 million statewide. On a $334 million bank, that is about 35 people instead of 49.

Thomasville National Bank is the extreme case: $2.18 billion with 167 employees and four offices, a 38.3% efficiency ratio, and about $154,000 per employee.

The overhead gap between the leaders and the median bank is 74 basis points of assets. On a $334 million bank, that is about $2.5 million a year.
