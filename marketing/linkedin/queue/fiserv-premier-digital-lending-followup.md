Fiserv markets a 30-day implementation window for AppMarket apps.

Read the fine print: that window is for cloud-hosted Premier. A bank running its core in its own data center should expect connectivity work and a statement of work first.

Three questions before any Fiserv lending demo:

Which core are you live on: Premier, Precision or Portico?
How many institutions on my core, hosted the way I am, are live today?
Does the decision book to the core automatically, or does someone rekey it?

The last one decides whether the project saves staff time or just moves it.
