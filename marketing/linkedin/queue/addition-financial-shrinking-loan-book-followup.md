Addition Financial has 104 fewer employees than the two credit unions that merged to form it a year ago, and 5,139 more members.

Addition and Envision Credit Union employed 854 people on September 30, 2025. Addition reported 750 on September 30, 2026, per NCUA call reports.

Credit is the watch item. Loans delinquent two months or more reached 1.30% of the book, up from 0.92% for the two combined a year earlier and above the 0.78% Florida credit union average at June 30.

The front door is split three ways: membership and consumer loans on MeridianLink, mortgages on a separate portal, business loans by appointment.

When headcount is already lean, where does the next efficiency gain in your lending operation come from?
