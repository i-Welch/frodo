Tampa's New South Bank just won FDIC approval for deposit insurance.

The FDIC approved the application with conditions on Sept. 28, four weeks after Florida's Office of Financial Regulation gave its conditional approval. The order requires at least $25 million in initial capital and expires if insurance is not effective within a year.

Other Southeast de novos crossed the finish line this fall. Nola Bank opened in New Orleans on Sept. 14. Portrait Bank opened to the public in Winter Park on Sept. 19. Private Bank & Trust opened in Gainesville, Georgia, on Sept. 28.

The pipeline is still growing. The FDIC has received 25 deposit insurance applications in 2026, and six of them are Florida banks, including filings from Itaú and Stark Bank in Miami.
