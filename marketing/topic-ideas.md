## Outreach targets

Only one domain made the top three on three or more of the 19 queries:

- **americanbanker.com** (4: de novo banks 2026, new bank charters Florida 2026, South Carolina de novo bank, Georgia bank rankings ROA). It is still the top pitch target. Offer the de novo beat De Novo Watch data and the national-count discrepancy above, and once PR #40 ships, offer the data desk the Georgia ROA read, since their ranked table in the Georgia results dates from 2001.

Domains that appeared anywhere in the results (not only the top three) for three or more queries, ranked by off-site value:

- **ICBA / Independent Banker** (icba.org, independentbanker.org; 4: digital account opening, community bank digital lending, account opening fraud, Florida charters). This is the association route in the playbook's Month 1 list: a contributed article or a member-vendor listing.
- **cuinsight.com** (4: digital account opening, digital loan application, borrower verification, credit union digital lending audit). It accepts contributed articles and press releases, so it is the credit union channel for the audit-data piece.
- **pcbb.com BID Daily** (3: de novo banks 2026, Florida charters, peer comparison tool). It writes about de novo formation and peer benchmarking, so pitch the same data.
- **fitgap.com** (3: MANTL alternatives, digital loan application, credit union digital lending audit). This is a software directory and listicle site; a free listing puts RAVEN into "alternatives" and "best LOS for credit unions" pages.

Software directories (sourceforge.net, slashdot.org, softwareone.com marketplace) each made the top three on two queries. A free listing is a cheap way into "Work Number alternatives" and "borrower verification software". Data directories (visbanking.com, BlastPoint) own the bank-data queries; they are competitors for `/bank-snapshot`, not targets.
