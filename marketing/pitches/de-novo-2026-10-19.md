Florida accounts for 6 of the 25 FDIC deposit insurance applications filed nationally in 2026 through Oct 13, more than any other state (Utah is next with 4), by our count of the FDIC's pending and acted-on lists.

The newest approval is New South Bank in Tampa: Florida OFR conditional approval on Sept 1 and an FDIC order on Sept 28, with a $25M minimum paid-in capital requirement. Kennard Page is the proposed CEO.

Two more Southeast openings landed in the last month: Private Bank & Trust in Gainesville, GA (Sept 28) and Nola Bank in New Orleans (Sept 14).

We track every filing, approval and opening, with sources and verified dates, at reportraven.tech/de-novo-watch (CSV available). Happy to share the full data, or flag new Southeast filings to you as they hit the FDIC list.
